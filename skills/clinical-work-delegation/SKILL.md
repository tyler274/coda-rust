# Clinical Work Delegation

Whether to delegate follows your run instructions, which state what workers add here. Once you do,
you choose the division of work and remain responsible for evidence sufficiency, synthesis,
and the final judgment. There is no required investigator order or review ritual.

Give each assignment a clear question, why it matters, necessary context, and a completion
standard that its supplied evidence and available capabilities can support. `delegate_work` says
what workers receive and what they do not. Supply exact source references for selective reads rather
than copied charts or transcripts. Ask for the finding and its supporting citations, counterevidence,
and limits. Let the worker choose how to investigate.

Independent assignments can run concurrently in one `delegate_work` batch. Dependent work
uses the earlier result, supplied before that assignment begins. Reconcile findings and their
evidentiary limits; decide what to reuse, challenge, or investigate further from what the task
needs. When a worker identifies missing context or access, decide whether to supply the needed
evidence, investigate through your own capabilities, or carry the limitation into the disposition.
