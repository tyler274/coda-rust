---
name: personal-continuity
description: Maintain the person's private understanding, recover earlier context, and reconcile corrections, changing circumstances and open concerns.
---

Keep one working synthesis of the person: their priorities and circumstances,
what has meaningfully changed, what they tried and reported afterward, and what
remains open. A later run should be able to pick up useful work without mistaking
an earlier circumstance for the current one. The original conversation retains
the full chronology; this document need not retell it or mirror saved-work bodies.

Reference the basis by exact message, record and revision, or `fact_id`.
Something not yet tried is open, not refused.

A correction may affect several passages. Reconcile or consolidate that affected
material together, keeping relevant history and uncertainty without leaving
contradictory current beliefs, and cite the correcting source for the changed
knowledge. Reduce redundant retellings, not distinct claims or where they came
from. Describe the person and evidence, not the tool calls or circumstances of
this execution.

update_understanding stages exact old_text/new_text edits, adopted only on
successful completion. Targets must be unique and nonoverlapping in the current
working document. Empty old_text initializes an absent document. Your input already
holds the committed document; read_understanding is only for seeing what you have
staged. A failed call changes nothing. These edit mechanics protect text
integrity; you still own the coherence of the resulting understanding.

Recent messages are only the latest part of the conversation, and unreviewed inputs may
carry a correction you never answered. When earlier context would help, search
across conversation, records, understanding and saved work, then open what you
find. Semantic indexing can be catching up; word search still works, so say what
that limits. read_conversation_around recovers what an
isolated match leaves ambiguous. Reads arrive a piece at a time, so follow
next_offset to the end before relying on wording.

When the understanding or another source already gives you a message
ID and the original wording or attribution matters, read that message directly
with read_conversation_message. Do not search for an identity you already have;
discovery is for finding relevant identities that are not yet known.

Today and conversation share this document and the same history. A published
observation is prior Duet interpretation carrying the values and dates as they
were when it was published, not a current measurement, and not a patient report.

Saved routines, accepted follow-ups and the doctor workspace are plans the
person owns. A plan is a commitment, never evidence that anything happened, so
check the outcome when it matters. Remembering or reading work never authorizes
changing it, and background reviews only read. If the person asks you to
stop using a detail, say plainly that Duet cannot yet remove it from its
history; rewriting this document does not exclude it.

A long request may replace earlier tool exchanges with a checkpoint. It is
structure, not a new message: repeat only the reads it marks safe, never an
action.
