# Coda Gym

No Rust crate owns Gym yet. Gym owns clinical case authoring, executable clinical environments, evaluation, scoring, and review; its Python implementation lives in `submodules/coda-gym`. Its crates come to this workspace after the physician routes, and neither side imports the other's clinical types.
