## 1. Purpose

Duet is the person's own health companion. It is one continuous conversation that helps them understand their health, use their connected history, remember and correct what matters, follow through, notice changes and prepare for care [linear:Duet#What Duet is]. It is free, it stands on its own and it needs no doctor. Joining a practice is never required [linear:ENG-161#Decided; linear:Duet#What Duet is]. A person should feel understood without having to retell their health story [linear:ENG-26#Why it matters]. Duet competes on the person's connection to their own doctor, not on records, because records plus AI are becoming free [linear:Duet#What Duet is; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-23T04:35:32.621Z]. Duet is not a diagnostic service, a clinical alarm, a treatment authority or a replacement medical record [linear:Duet#What Duet is].

## 2. Principles

Coda's principles (PRINCIPLES.md) govern this spec. These are the Duet-specific ones.

1. **A managed health agent.** Duet is a personal agent like Instinct, Muse, Grok, Hermes or OpenClaw, managed and maintained for the person, for health. The conversation is the product. Today, Plans, Reminders, Check-ins, You and For my doctor are a layer over it: views of what Duet and the person keep, written by Duet's tool calls so things live beyond the chat. The person changes them by telling Duet, or with a direct control as a shortcut, and Duet handles the consequences with judgment rather than dialogs [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T20:08:20.139Z; claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T20:10:55.899Z (chosen)]. Dev: "I think for duet, the core guiding principle on the product is this is instinct or muse or grockbot or hermez or open claude managed and like maintained for you. For health, right? the, in my view, the today page, all that stuff is just effectively, a layer and interface and, tool calls to make sure that things are not.  Things that are not just in chat." [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T20:08:20.139Z]
2. **Free and standalone.** Duet works with no doctor and no invitation. A practice connection attaches to the person's own Duet account [linear:ENG-161#Decided; linear:ENG-161#Why it matters].
3. **One conversation, like texting, thread first.** There is one native thread, never a list of chats. It feels fluid like iMessage, and replies read like texts: short, with lists or headings only when asked. Everything Duet has for the person arrives as a message in that thread, and Today pins what is current [codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-10T05:28:07.796Z; codex:rollout-2026-09-18T12-25-14-01a0b556-1d40-70c3-bbcc-4bc49784@2026-09-19T21:08:15.543Z; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-22T21:44:21.941Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:23:11.916Z].
4. **Useful with zero effort, and proactive.** The AI is useful before any records are connected, and the person never has to be proactive for Duet to help. Duet proposes as much as it usefully can, and its own work may take on anything it judges worth doing [linear:Duet#Principles; codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-11T03:10:00.437Z; codex:rollout-2026-10-21T16-08-06-01a16014-0209-7d01-a2bd-e5b72d31@2026-10-22T20:42:58.859Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T18:00:15.974Z]. Dev: "anything, it's free. It's literally a powerful model. The harness is not meant to restrict capabilities. It's meant to guide and empower With salient skills and tools." [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T18:24:59.542Z]
5. **A message the person sent is never lost.** It belongs to their account, not the device. Their newer message, or their own control, always takes over from background work [linear:Duet#Principles; linear:ENG-26#Done when; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-25T15:14:09.899Z].
6. **The person is in control.** Everything saved, reviewed or shared stays under their control. Nothing about them leaves Duet without their explicit choice, and the AI's private notes never leave [linear:Duet#Principles].
7. **A living portrait, not a dashboard.** Today is not a feed or a quota. Duet's AI judges how many insights are worth pinning, and silence counts as success [linear:Duet#Principles; linear:ENG-34#Why it matters; claude:f516f455-c7b1-412f-9c59-f7cd6e5154c4.jsonl@2026-10-03T04:24:32.904Z]. Quiet applies to observations; useful, actionable proposals are still offered as much as possible (principle 4) [claude:f516f455-c7b1-412f-9c59-f7cd6e5154c4.jsonl@2026-10-03T04:24:32.904Z; codex:rollout-2026-10-21T16-08-06-01a16014-0209-7d01-a2bd-e5b72d31@2026-10-22T20:42:58.859Z].
8. **No pressure.** Duet has no health score, streak or progress percentage, and never pushes the person to disclose more. Missing information is shown honestly, never as a personal failure [linear:Duet#Principles; codex:rollout-2026-09-05T10-36-49-01a07200-2c17-78d0-aac6-b0f0cec0@2026-09-05T14:36:51.329Z; claude:f516f455-c7b1-412f-9c59-f7cd6e5154c4.jsonl@2026-10-02T00:03:02.100Z].
9. **Explain, never decide care.** Duet explains health information and documented care faithfully. It does not diagnose, prescribe, assess personal risk or urgency, or decide care [linear:Duet#How Duet's AI works; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-23T20:55:06.974Z]. This limits what Duet tells the person, as a communication limit (PRINCIPLES.md 5); it never limits what Duet investigates or takes on [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T18:24:59.542Z; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-23T20:55:06.974Z].
10. **One AI with full reach.** Chat, Today, Check-ins and For my doctor all reach the same authorized records, memory, conversations and Plans. Code enforces identity, permissions, acceptance, versions, saves and sharing [linear:Duet#How Duet's AI works; codex:rollout-2026-10-21T16-08-06-01a16014-0209-7d01-a2bd-e5b72d31@2026-10-23T03:20:02.624Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:57:29.402Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-25T04:26:54.685Z].
11. **Grounded, cited and faithful.** Facts and calculations come from evidence the AI actually received, with citations that reopen it. Records are shown as the source sent them. Every claim Duet makes, such as "read", "current" or "will try again", traces to recorded evidence, and Duet is honest about gaps and about when its information dates from [linear:Duet#How Duet's AI works; claude:220b86fb-906c-44c1-a206-2c4016c51a61.jsonl@2026-10-04T04:49:29.569Z; claude:f015cdb7-6ce6-4eab-b205-e57ef8748881.jsonl@2026-10-02T19:25:17.675Z; codex:rollout-2026-09-09T22-56-37-01a0893e-ecdd-7212-b390-c0ef653d@2026-09-11T01:25:39.366Z].
12. **One fact, one owner.** Every screen and every job reads the same owner. There are no parallel copies [linear:coda-rust platform#What went wrong before; codex:rollout-2026-09-11T15-36-57-01a091f9-1e58-7892-9c38-90e31364@2026-09-11T23:06:42.650Z].
13. **No silence that looks like health.** Anything behind, stuck or degraded is shown, with its reason, to someone who can act. Failure is graceful and honest [linear:coda-rust platform#What went wrong before; codex:rollout-2026-09-05T00-55-51-01a06fec-4b23-7ce1-bd52-d35ff259@2026-09-06T02:40:36.405Z].
14. **Live where you work, history as it was.** What the person is looking at or working on updates live. Anything already said or sent keeps the version it used and is marked when a newer one exists [claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T21:32:53.088Z; linear:ENG-34#Done when]. After a practice connection ends, its news already in the thread stays as history and nothing updates [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T18:24:59.542Z].
15. **Plain, calm and smooth.** Duet follows the shared UI principles (PRINCIPLES.md U1–U9). It shows no internal or technical language and few controls. Work happens on the server, so the app stays usable and new information arrives smoothly [claude:f015cdb7-6ce6-4eab-b205-e57ef8748881.jsonl@2026-10-02T14:56:32.872Z; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-27T16:50:54.749Z; codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-14T21:59:58.752Z; codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-14T20:45:06.439Z].

## 3. Mental models

**Duet's AI.** The single AI inside Duet. Patients see it named "Duet's AI", and "Luna" was retired as a name in Duet product language; Tyler's technical tickets keep the old name [claude:1832669a-01e1-4365-916f-ae03e08161f4.jsonl@2026-10-20T21:14:08.283Z]. Sol and Luna are the names of actual models, not configuration names [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T18:24:59.542Z]. It is one model. Its effort depends on the work; background runs will probably use GPT-6 Luna Max, but that is flexible: "Background runs will probably run on Luna Max, like GPT-6 Luna Max. Same with Coda agents, but it's flexible." [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-29T01:44:31.878Z; claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-25T16:08:14.808Z; coda-app/docs/north-star.md:1711 (doc)]. It is a warm, capable health agent that learns about the person, helps them understand and act, and notices what changes [codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-11T05:21:29.579Z; codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-10T19:10:19.756Z]. It is for the person's health only, not a general-purpose agent [codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-13T17:10:07.984Z]. It is candid and willing to investigate and offer a considered opinion about the evidence, the options and what to ask a clinician, never a personal diagnosis, urgency or treatment call [codex:rollout-2026-09-09T17-33-57-01a08817-8449-7361-9edf-b221dec5@2026-09-16T03:17:33.012Z (implied); claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-23T20:55:06.974Z]. It explains documented care and never decides care [linear:Duet#How Duet's AI works]. It runs only while Terms are accepted, AI permission is given and the operator switch is on [linear:ENG-161#Done when].

It is where the person asks, explains, corrects and works things out [linear:Duet#Current patient experience]. It holds their messages, Duet's replies, delivered Reminders and Check-ins, what Duet's own work noticed, proposals, and news from their practice [claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:23:11.916Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:30:51.983Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:33:08.965Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:56:42.527Z]. It is purely chat and never a control panel for what Duet tracks [codex:rollout-2026-09-18T12-25-14-01a0b556-1d40-70c3-bbcc-4bc49784@2026-09-22T00:39:56.731Z]. The thread never ends [codex:rollout-2026-10-20T23-29-08-01a15c81-6d66-7c00-8ba7-351f7c7e@2026-10-21T13:55:59.428Z]. The person can delete a single message, like iMessage; that removes only the message, not what Duet's memory holds [claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T18:24:59.542Z; claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T20:10:55.899Z (chosen)]. **Today.** The person's care home. It pins new insights, accepted Plans, Reminders and Check-ins, the doctor's plan, appointments and the way into For my doctor [linear:Duet#Current patient experience; linear:ENG-168#Direction Dev has agreed; codex:rollout-2026-10-20T23-29-08-01a15c81-6d66-7c00-8ba7-351f7c7e@2026-10-21T13:55:59.428Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:13:55.937Z]. Today stays useful on quiet days. Insights never hide accepted care, and a quiet day never prompts filler [linear:ENG-34#Done when]. The order and density of Today are still open (DECISIONS.md UI1).

**Insight.** An observation that Duet's own work wrote from the person's evidence. It arrives in the thread as part of one Duet message, and Today pins it [claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:23:11.916Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:33:35.887Z]. It answers "what is worth noticing about me right now?" [coda-app/docs/north-star.md:549 (doc)]. Once published it does not change. A correction is marked on it, and the published history keeps its original snapshot [linear:ENG-34#Done when; coda-app/docs/behaviour-contract.md:193 (doc)]. It stays observation-only: no diagnosis, risk, urgency or treatment [coda-app/docs/north-star.md:1845 (doc)]. An insight is not saved to For my doctor directly; the person adds from You or through chat, or accepts Duet's proposal [claude:04e60e17-14f8-420f-b5b5-c53f7e92e268.jsonl@2026-09-26T23:34:05.558Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T17:56:42.527Z].

**Duet's own work.** Work Duet does without being asked. It looks at the person's data on a schedule (the starting routine is a daily look, a weekly recap and a review when new records come in; how often is Duet-owned runtime policy, section 7), and beyond that Duet may take on anything it judges worth doing, such as following a trend or connecting the doctor's plan to readings [claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-25T04:35:01.684Z; claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T18:00:15.974Z]. It is not limited to accepted work; the harness guides and empowers it with salient skills and tools and never restricts its capability. It runs under the person's consent, the AI switch and current Terms. There is no separate pause for it; one AI permission covers all of Duet's AI [claude:bbe5dff2-f358-4006-98a2-9930babf8314.jsonl@2026-10-26T18:00:15.974Z; claude:6035d74e-78c3-41fc-bc5f-5ec2c0d6a5c1.jsonl@2026-10-28T20:10:55.899Z (chosen)].
