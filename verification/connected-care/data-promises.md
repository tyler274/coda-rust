# Data promises

Promises about records that the charts' states can't hold. The composition
([connection.mmd](connection.mmd)) and both graphs hold the states around each
one; the record itself is the Rust owner's, and that owner is where each must be
proved. None is proved yet. That the live feed reaches the practice only while
connected is the composition's rules, proved in `verus/`.

## For my doctor reaches the practice only as the patient sent it

**Promise.** For my doctor goes to the practice only when the patient sends it,
as the version saved then, while both sides show them connected; later saves
don't change what was sent (Duet decisions.md, "Your practice"; Dev 2026-10-26).

**Graph.** Duet's "your Send to my practice on For my doctor goes through" =
the physician graph's "the patient sends For my doctor".

**Rust owner.** None yet.
