## Resolutions (R)

1. **The PFT and "no doctor in over a year".** The source has a PFT six months
   before the 2023-10-13 visit and no clinician visit for over a year. The PCP
   ordered the PFT and the pulmonology referral at the 2022-09-08 physical (A1); the
   PFT was done 2023-04-12 at the pulmonology group's lab, a test without a
   clinician visit, and he saw the pulmonologist only on 2023-10-13 when symptoms
   worsened. The source gives no PFT numbers; the values in the timeline carry
   "reversible obstruction" and are the only ones used. No severity word appears on
   the report.
2. **One asthma condition and an unfinished plan.** "Asthma, mild persistent
   (childhood onset)" and "Mild persistent asthma (diagnosed 2020-01-15)" are one
   condition, mild persistent asthma, on the list from joining with childhood onset;
   the "diagnosed" stamp is not carried. The pulmonology plan stops at "a change in
   asthma severity": the letter states that assessment, continues albuterol as
   needed, advises limiting dust and fume exposure at work, and leaves follow-up
   with pulmonology as needed. The problem's label does not change.
3. **The occupational medicine vignette.** The source's "unit", "technician" and
   "transporting sterile equipment" are hospital quiz text; the chart makes his
   part-time warehouse a medical-supply distributor, places the influenza cluster
   among its staff, and drops the quiz question. The source's "peak flu season" in
   October becomes early in the season; "prior influenza in July 2021" becomes March
   2025.
4. **Occupational exposure.** The source comorbidity "Occupational exposure to
   hazardous substances" is recorded once, at the 2023-10-13 pulmonology letter,
   which first documents the dust and fume exposure (ICD-10 Z57.5).
5. **Allergy list.** The source lists only dust mites and seasonal pollen; the
   chart records them as environmental allergies without a reaction and adds no
   known drug allergies.
6. **Ages and history.** Ages follow the birth date: 34 at pulmonology, 36 at the ED
   and occupational medicine (the source says 35 at the ED). The ED note's "denies
   any significant past medical history" contradicts his asthma and is dropped. The
   ED note's back-reference to pulmonology follows the re-dated visit (October 2023).

**Left as the source has it:** no controller medication is started at or after the
